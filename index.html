How to play

Aim the cannon, pick your power, FIRE! Bounce as far as you can, grab Scrap Coins and upgrade in the Garage.

Aim: drag on the game or use the sliders. Keys: UP/DOWN angle, LEFT/RIGHT power, SPACE fires.
In flight: hold LEFT / RIGHT to nudge, DOWN to dive (touch: arrow buttons). P pauses, R retries.

Springs launch, tyres bounce, fans blow, red barrels blast you forward, rusty walls block.
